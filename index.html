A LITTLE CLARITY

It’s okay to
have questions.

Don’t see yours here?
We’re happy to help.

Are all appointments virtual?

Yes. Live Well Counseling is a completely virtual telehealth practice. Meet with your provider from the convenience of your own space, anywhere in Ohio.

How do I choose the right provider?

Each clinician has different specialties and experience. Explore the team’s biographies, then reach out to request an appointment with the provider who feels like a good fit.

Do you accept my insurance?

Our practice lists Aetna, Anthem Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Ohio, Buckeye, CareSource, Cigna, Medical Mutual, Molina, Ohio Medicaid, UMR, and United HealthCare. Confirm your specific plan and provider’s participation with us before booking.

Can I receive medication management?

Yes, we offer psychiatric services and medication management. Contact the practice to learn about available care options.

What if I need immediate help?

This website is not an emergency service. For mental health emergencies, call 911 or 988, or visit your nearest hospital.
